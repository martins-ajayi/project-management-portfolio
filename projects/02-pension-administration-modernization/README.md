**Delivery focus:** Governed hybrid implementation

**Illustrative reconstruction based on résumé-described responsibilities.** Budgets, dates, scale, incidents, decisions and completion evidence are fictional portfolio examples, not verified historical results. No employer or client records are reproduced.

## Delivery question
A reconciliation defect blocks release even though the aggregate UAT pass rate reaches 95%. The scenario shows an approved reserve draw, a visible one-week forecast change and independent Finance acceptance.

The project domain and broad coordination responsibilities come from his supplied résumé. Supporting documents are newly reconstructed to demonstrate delivery methods. They are not contemporaneous employer records or independent verification of personal accomplishments.
