## Test strategy and ownership
QA coordinates functional, integration, boundary-rule, negative-access and migration tests. Business Owner approves business acceptance; Finance signs reconciliation; Technical Lead confirms remediation; Operations accepts restore and runbook evidence. PM coordinates the gate, preserving independent assurance.

UAT denominator is the full planned suite. Pass rate = passed / planned; failed, blocked and unexecuted cases remain in the denominator. Zero P0/P1 and passing evidence for every Must requirement are required in addition to ≥95% pass rate.
