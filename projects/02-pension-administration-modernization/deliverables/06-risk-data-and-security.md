## Active risk control
Risks are scored likelihood × impact on illustrative 1–5 scales; ≥15 High, 8–14 Medium, ≤7 Low. Issues are managed by urgency and severity rather than a fabricated probability score. See [RAID](../registers/raid.csv).

At W24, risk R-P01 materializes as I-P01: an identifier mapping joins a service-history row to the wrong synthetic pensioner. The team freezes affected migration output, preserves original fixtures and reconciles every exception. The PM reports the gate impact and asks for recovery authority.

## Data governance plan
1. Business and Finance approve source-to-target mapping and rule inventory.
2. Data Lead profiles duplicates, missing keys and inconsistent dates before loading.
3. Rehearsals use synthetic records and reproducible control totals.
4. Accepted and quarantined records reconcile to every source row. Exceptions have an owner and signed disposition; nothing silently disappears.
5. Finance validates benefit values and approval-state totals separately from row counts.
6. The team retains lineage and a reconciliation report for independent gate review.

In the successful W26 reconstruction, 12,000 source records = 11,988 accepted + 12 quarantined. All 12 quarantines have approved remediation owners; none is treated as an active eligible payment record. Accepted record values have zero unexplained control-total variance.

Least-privilege roles, maker-checker separation, audit logging, secure test environments, retention and restore controls require review by responsible specialists. The portfolio does not claim compliance with a named statute; applicable obligations require identification and specialist approval in a real project.
