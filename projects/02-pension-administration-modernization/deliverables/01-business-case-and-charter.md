# Pension Administration Modernization

**Illustrative reconstruction based on résumé-described responsibilities.** Budgets, dates, scale, incidents, decisions and completion evidence are fictional portfolio examples, not verified historical results. No employer or client records are reproduced.

## Decision and business purpose
Authorize a controlled transition of pension administration records and workflows. The business needs consistent beneficiary registration, service-history records, eligibility and calculation review, approval controls and payment tracking. The reconstruction illustrates how a project manager coordinates specialists while protecting data integrity.

The résumé supports the domain and coordination responsibilities. It does not establish a particular software product, migration volume, financial ceiling or legal jurisdiction. Those details below are scenario assumptions.

## Options and recommendation
| Option | Value | Delivery concern | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Retain existing workflows | Minimal immediate disruption | Fragmented records and approval visibility remain | Reject for this scenario |
| Configure a packaged platform | Potentially faster implementation | Requires validated fit, vendor capability and integration evidence | Keep as a discovery option |
| Deliver a tailored administration platform | Supports specific eligibility and approval workflows | More design and assurance effort | Select after W6 fit review in this scenario |

No real procurement outcome or product expertise is implied. The PM coordinates option evaluation; pension policy and solution specialists own rule interpretation and design.

## Scenario authorization
32 weeks; CAD 480,000 funding ceiling, comprising CAD 450,000 base work and CAD 30,000 contingency. The migration rehearsal uses 12,000 synthetic pensioner records. Pilot production access is limited to an illustrative administration unit; no real pension payments are executed by this portfolio.

In scope: beneficiary and service records, approved eligibility/calculation rules, maker-checker workflow, role access, audit events, migration, payment-status interface validation, training and support transition. Out of scope: changing pension policy, actuarial advice, direct bank disbursement, biometric capture and replacing financial ledgers.

## Success and authority
All Must requirements have passing evidence. All source rows are accepted or quarantined with disposition; no unexplained reconciliation variance. Zero P0/P1 at release. Sponsor, Business Owner, Finance Controller and Operations approve their respective gate evidence. The Sponsor makes the final release decision. PM can coordinate recovery inside the baseline but cannot waive integrity controls or approve funding.
