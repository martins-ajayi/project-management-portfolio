# Pension Administration Modernization

**Illustrative reconstruction based on résumé-described responsibilities.** Budgets, dates, scale, incidents, decisions and completion evidence are fictional portfolio examples, not verified historical results. No employer or client records are reproduced.

## Governance and engagement
| Role | Concern | Engagement and decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sponsor | Funding and business outcomes | Fortnightly steering; exception briefing within one working day |
| Business Owner | Eligibility and beneficiary accuracy | Weekly rule workshop; approves business acceptance |
| Finance Controller | Calculations and reconciliations | Rehearsal reviews; approves finance controls |
| Data Lead | Mapping, exceptions and lineage | Weekly migration forum; daily during recovery |
| QA Lead | Coverage and defect evidence | Daily UAT triage; independent gate recommendation |
| Technical Lead | Integration and access controls | Daily delivery coordination; design authority |
| Operations Lead | Support, restore and access administration | Weekly readiness from W20; accepts live service |
| Administrators | Usable workflows and training | Role-based walkthroughs and scenario UAT |

## RACI with one accountable role per activity
| Activity | Accountable | Responsible | Consulted | Informed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Approve funding and baseline | Sponsor | PM | Business Owner, Finance, Tech | Team |
| Approve eligibility rules | Business Owner | BA | Finance, Policy SME | PM, QA |
| Approve finance reconciliation | Finance Controller | Data Lead | Business Owner, QA | Sponsor, PM |
| Maintain integrated plan and RAID | PM | PM | Workstream leads | Sponsor |
| Deliver technical solution | Technical Lead | Engineers | Data, Security, QA | PM |
| Accept UAT | Business Owner | QA | Finance, administrators | PM, Sponsor |
| Authorize release | Sponsor | PM | Business, Finance, QA, Ops | Team |
| Accept operational service | Operations Lead | Support team | Business, Tech, QA | Sponsor, PM |

Critical data or access events escalate immediately. Decisions record alternatives, affected baseline, authority and follow-up. Stakeholder communications distinguish forecast from approved commitment. Role labels are fictional assignments rather than names of actual participants.
