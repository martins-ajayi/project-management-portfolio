# Network Management Application Design and Delivery

**Illustrative reconstruction based on résumé-described responsibilities.** Budgets, dates, scale, incidents, decisions and completion evidence are fictional portfolio examples, not verified historical results. No employer or client records are reproduced.

## Business problem and authorization
Customers struggle to locate network-management features and complete common tasks. The initiative explores user needs and designs a Wi-Fi management application experience. The résumé describes cross-functional design leadership, desirability research, competitor comparison, current-state analysis, interviews, journey mapping, design proposals, UX/UI cycles and usability testing.

This reconstruction ends at an accepted prototype and implementation handoff. It does not claim a production app launch, network firmware delivery or live customer improvements.

## Options and scenario baseline
| Option | Assessment | Scenario decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adjust labels in existing navigation | Low effort but weak evidence of task discovery | Test during discovery |
| Redesign priority journeys with a prototype | Enables comparative task testing before build | Select |
| Commission a full production rebuild immediately | High commitment before user validation | Defer until design gate |

16-week scenario; CAD 120,000 ceiling comprising CAD 112,000 base work and CAD 8,000 reserve. Team roles: PM, Product Owner, Research Lead, UX Lead, Visual/Content Lead, Technical Lead and QA/Accessibility Reviewer. Twelve fictional participants in each baseline/final research round; repeated participants may introduce learning bias and must be recorded.

In scope: priority journeys, feature desirability, current-state review, comparator study, content/navigation, interactive prototype, usability evidence and implementation handoff. Out: backend build, firmware, live network provisioning, app-store release and production adoption metrics.

Success: each priority journey ≥80% task completion in the final scenario round, zero unresolved critical usability/privacy findings, documented accessibility review and receiving-team acceptance. Small-sample results guide design and are not population estimates or proof of real business impact.
