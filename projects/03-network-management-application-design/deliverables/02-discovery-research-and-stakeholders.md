# Network Management Application Design and Delivery

**Illustrative reconstruction based on résumé-described responsibilities.** Budgets, dates, scale, incidents, decisions and completion evidence are fictional portfolio examples, not verified historical results. No employer or client records are reproduced.

## Discovery sequence
W1–W2: clarify sponsor outcome and current-state problem. W3–W4: interviews, task inventory, journey mapping and comparative feature review. W5: Product Owner selects priority journeys. W6–W11: iterative concepts and prototype testing. W12–W14: final usability and accessibility checks. W15–W16: handoff and closure.

Research Lead defines recruitment criteria, script and consent process; avoids leading questions and captures task attempts consistently. Use synthetic device/customer details and consented nonidentifying notes. No real interview transcripts or customer data are included.
