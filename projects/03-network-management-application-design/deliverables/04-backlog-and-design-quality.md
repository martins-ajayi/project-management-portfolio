## Iteration plan and acceptance controls
Two-week design cycles W6–W7, W8–W9 and W10–W11. Each review records accepted, revise or deferred. Product Owner orders work; designers estimate; PM tracks capacity and research dependencies. Story-point velocity is not used because no verified throughput history exists.

Definition of ready: priority task and user need stated, prototype acceptance condition, feasibility reviewer and research method identified. Definition of done: versioned interaction, agreed content, error states, keyboard/focus review, feasibility notes and testable handoff acceptance. Passing a prototype task is distinct from a production service test.
