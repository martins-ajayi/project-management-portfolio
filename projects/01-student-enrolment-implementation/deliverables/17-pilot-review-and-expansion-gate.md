**Reconstructed example; no real operational results or approvals are asserted.**

## Purpose

Decide whether the 10-school pilot launched in W18 provides adequate evidence to open the W21 expansion to 40 additional schools. Deployment alone is not sufficient; review performance, support load, accessibility, data reconciliation, training and critical defects.

## Decision options presented to sponsor

A. **Expand:** evidence passes, maintain monitoring and support for noncritical issues. B. **Conditionally expand:** add safeguards if one low/medium threshold is borderline. C. **Defer:** any unresolved P0/P1 or material integrity, adoption or support problem.

**Simulated DEC-04:** Sponsor selects **A** at W20, allowing the W21 expansion. Operations Lead accepts incident responsibilities; PM communicates plan and decision to stakeholders; Change Lead confirms training wave. No unsupported claim about an actual rollout is made.

## Post-gate controls

Continue daily operational pulse during expansion, monitor source-data exceptions, staff support, and registration completions; maintain rollback triggers. W24 closure requires documented residual ownership and benefits measurement, not simply the W21 release milestone.
