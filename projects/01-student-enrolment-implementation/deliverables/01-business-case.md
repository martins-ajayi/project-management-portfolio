# 01 | Business case, options and benefits hypothesis

**Version:** 0.9 — illustrative reconstruction | **Gate:** Sponsor authorizes W2 concept, conditional on W5 baseline approval

## Problem to solve

The enrolment workflow requires school representatives to submit candidates, validate eligibility, select examinations and receive confirmation. Manual hand-offs increase incomplete records, re-entry effort and reconciliation concerns. The business needs one governed registration process with auditable records and a managed support model.

## Outcomes, not features

- Reduce failed/incomplete eligible registrations (illustrative target ≥95% completion).
- Make registration validation consistent and auditable.
- Improve operational visibility into submission status and exceptions.
- Introduce repeatable training and support practices at schools.

## Options assessment — qualitative, scenario-only

| Option | Delivery / control assessment | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Keep the current process | Lowest immediate change, but fragmented controls persist | Reject |
| B. Configure a generic third-party portal | Faster start, but fit and compliance assumptions require validation | Reserve option |
| **C. Implement a tailored enrolment platform** | Better alignment to validation workflows; more testing and delivery ownership | **Recommend** |

**Recommendation rationale:** Option C enables defined business rules and integrated acceptance criteria. This is not a verified historical procurement or product decision; it demonstrates investment reasoning.

## Financial boundary

**Illustrative investment ceiling:** CAD 240,000 inclusive of CAD 12,000 contingency. No historic ROI, savings or payback figures are available from the resume; therefore no ROI is claimed. Benefits are treated as **hypotheses for post-release measurement**, not proven benefits.

Sponsor authorizes discovery at W2. Full delivery is conditioned on W5 sign-off of minimum viable scope, delivery capacity, privacy/access requirements, schedule, acceptance strategy, and contingency ownership. The sponsor retains rights to stop or re-scope the work at a gate.
