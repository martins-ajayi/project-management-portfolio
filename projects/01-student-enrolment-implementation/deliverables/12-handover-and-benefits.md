## Benefits realization management

**Benefits owner:** Business Owner. **Reporting handoff:** Operations Lead. **Checkpoints:** 30, 60 and 90 days after deployment (relative to release, not asserted historical dates). Measure success rate from eligible registrations and exception volume; separate seasonal effects from product performance where possible. Close the project but keep benefits measurement accountable.

## Lessons learned — simulated and transferable

1. Profile candidate IDs early, before migration dry-runs become release-critical.
2. Retain gate integrity: a missed quality threshold is Amber/Red, even when stakeholders want a Green headline.
3. Maintain scenario-based contingency controls linked to a specific decision reference.
4. Pilot with explicit exit evidence before expansion, not merely elapsed time.
5. Train support teams ahead of launch; operational acceptance is distinct from functional acceptance.
