## Cost-control rules

- Workstream leads forecast resource needs and committed costs weekly.
- PM consolidates actuals/commitments/estimate-to-complete into cost forecast; sponsor sees exception trends.
- **Contingency is not free spending:** Sponsor approves any draw against the $12,000 risk reserve with associated reference in the decision log.
- Unused contingency remains unspent; scope changes requiring more than the $240,000 ceiling return to sponsor authorization.
