**Illustrative reconstruction based on résumé-described responsibilities.** Budgets, dates, scale, incidents, decisions and completion evidence are fictional portfolio examples, not verified historical results. No employer or client records are reproduced.

## Reconstructed management examples
Every specific budget, week, sample size, quantitative outcome, incident, approval, meeting and gate record is illustrative unless explicitly replaced by a confirmed fact. A label applies to the entire case and is repeated beside consequential figures. Scenario evidence tokens are references inside the example, not claims that real logs or signatures are attached.

The network case is a design/prototype initiative because the résumé establishes design and usability work; it does not establish production launch. Other cases likewise separate described responsibilities from invented execution detail.
