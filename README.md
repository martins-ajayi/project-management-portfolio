**Project Manager · PMP · Professional Scrum Master · Enterprise Systems and Digital Transformation**

With several years of experience in business systems and software delivery, I coordinate scope, stakeholders, teams, testing and implementation readiness. This portfolio shows how I structure decisions, manage dependencies and connect acceptance evidence to handover.

## Practical project management toolkit
[Templates and release evidence checker](https://github.com/martins-ajayi/project-management-toolkit) support chartering, scope, RACI, schedule, cost, RAID, requirements, changes, reporting, UAT, readiness and closure. The checker flags missing or failed mandatory evidence and includes reproducible examples and tests.
